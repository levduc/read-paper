# Default score scale

To use the scale of a venue, put a `score-scale.md` in the paper folder.
That file replaces this one.

## Overall merit

1. Reject: below the bar. The flaws or the lack of novelty decide it.
2. Weak reject: borderline, leaning to reject.
3. Weak accept: borderline, leaning to accept.
4. Accept: a clear contribution, with fixable issues.
5. Strong accept: a top paper of the venue.

## Confidence

1. A guess: the topic is outside my area.
2. Some familiarity: I may miss important points.
3. Fairly sure: I know the area, but I did not check every detail.
4. Sure: I checked the main claims and the closest prior work.
5. Certain: I checked the proofs or the numbers in full.
